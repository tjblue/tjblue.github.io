Lunch · Meal Prep · 7 Servings

Chipotle
Chicken Bowl

Baked thighs · cilantro rice · black beans · fajita veggies

70g Protein 115g Carbs 28g Fat 1,000 kcal
Servings
All amounts scale automatically
7
Ingredients
Instructions
1
Marinate the Chicken
⏱ 15 min
Combine olive oil, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and lime juice in a large bowl. Add chicken thighs and toss to coat. Let marinate while you prep everything else — or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavor.
2
Cook the Rice
⏱ 18 min
Combine white rice and chicken broth in a large pot. Bring to a boil, reduce to low, cover and simmer until fully absorbed and fluffy. Fluff with a fork and stir in half the cilantro.
3
Cook the Fajita Veggies
⏱ 10 min
Heat a large skillet or cast iron over medium-high heat. Add bell peppers and onions with a pinch of salt. Cook stirring occasionally until softened and slightly charred. Set aside.
4
Bake the Chicken
⏱ 25–30 min
Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large sheet pan with foil or parchment. Spread all marinated chicken thighs in a single layer — no batching needed. Bake 25–30 minutes until internal temp reaches 165°F. Rest 5 minutes then slice or chop.
5
Warm the Beans
⏱ 5 min
In a small pot, warm black beans over medium heat with a pinch of cumin and salt until heated through.
6
Assemble Containers
⏱ 10 min
Divide evenly across all containers: rice first, then chicken, beans, and fajita veggies. Top with shredded cheese and remaining cilantro. Squeeze extra lime if desired. Cool fully before sealing.
Storage & Reheating
🧊 Fridge: up to 5 days sealed
❄️ Freeze meals 6–7 if needed
🔥 Reheat 2–3 min microwave with splash of water
🌶 Add salsa or hot sauce — zero extra macros
🐔 Swap thighs for breast to cut ~5g fat
